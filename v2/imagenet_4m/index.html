Does the picture match the caption?

56 quick rounds, about seven minutes. Each round shows one caption and one picture. 1 Say whether the picture shows the caption, and 2 rate how good the picture looks, from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).

Does the picture match the caption?

  • Most captions name two objects and where they are (“a cat to the left of a dog”). Then only where things are matters, not how nice the picture looks. If an object is missing, the picture does not show the caption.
  • Some captions name just one thing (“a violin”). Then only whether that thing is in the picture matters.
  • For question 2, rate how good the picture looks, not where things are.
  • The models are still in the middle of training, so pictures are not perfect yet, e.g., odd faces or blurry objects. Just do your best to judge them.

The pictures were drawn by two AI image models. Which model drew each picture is hidden.
